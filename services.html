Services & Products

We deliver end-to-end product development across mobile, web and emerging surfaces — from first sketch to long-term maintenance. Our portfolio combines published App Store products with bespoke engineering for clients across the EU, UK, US and APAC.

// PUBLISHED APPS

Six apps. One philosophy: your data stays local.

Each app below is available on the Apple App Store, Google Play, and select alternative stores. All honour the same local-first, privacy-by-architecture principles.

iOS · ANDROID · iPADOS

Local Workflow Automation

Chain native iOS and Android actions into powerful on-device scripts. Build flows that read the screen, parse data, transform content, and trigger real actions across apps — all without sending a single byte to the cloud.

  • 40+ native triggers and actions (notifications, clipboard, files, calendar, etc.)
  • Visual flow editor with live debugging and execution traces
  • JavaScript and Python expression nodes for advanced logic
  • End-to-end encrypted optional sync via iCloud Drive / your own server
  • Apple Shortcuts and Android Intents bridge
iOS · iPADOS · MAC · WEB

Collectibles Value Engine

A focused portfolio tracker for serious collectors. Model trends, run projections, and capture provenance. Market data updates against a curated offline index so you can value items even on a plane.

  • Multi-category support: cards, coins, watches, sneakers, wine, art, memorabilia
  • Local valuation engine using time-series models
  • CSV / JSON import & export for tax and insurance
  • Encrypted vault for provenance documents and certificates
  • Provenance timeline with photographic evidence chain
iOS · WATCHOS

Posture Training Tracker

Sensor-driven, privacy-aware posture coaching. On-device machine-learning models infer sitting, standing, walking, and slouch states from motion and pressure data — without ever leaving your device.

  • Real-time posture nudges via haptic feedback
  • Daily and weekly progress graphs
  • Apple Health & Health Connect integration
  • Personalised routines built by a physiotherapist
  • Voice-coach mode (on-device text-to-speech)
iOS · ANDROID · TABLET

House Document Vault

Encrypted storage for the documents that keep your home running — deeds, contracts, warranties, insurance, and renovation records. Biometric unlock, automatic reminders and full-text search.

  • Biometric unlock (Face ID, Touch ID, Android Biometric)
  • AES-256-GCM encryption with per-document keys
  • Reminders for warranty expiry, insurance renewal, mortgage payments
  • OCR search across all PDFs and images
  • Secure share with expiring links and watermarking
iOS · ANDROID · WEB

Inspiration Fragmenter

Capture fleeting ideas — voice, photo, sketch, link — and let the app tag, group and resurface them. Built around the principle that creativity is mostly capture and recall, not organisation.

  • Multi-modal capture: text, voice, photo, sketch, link, screenshot
  • On-device AI clustering and tagging
  • Cross-device E2EE sync via your own storage (iCloud, Dropbox, WebDAV)
  • Daily resurfacing of forgotten fragments
  • Markdown export and PDF zine generation
iOS · ANDROID

Period Budget Engine

Recurring budget cycles with smart forecasting, envelope budgeting and a zero-cloud ledger. We treat personal finance as a tool, not a data-extraction opportunity.

  • Multi-currency with historical rates
  • Recurring and split transactions
  • Predictive cashflow insights using on-device ML
  • Plain-text ledger export for portability
  • Encrypted database with optional iCloud backup
