// WHO WE ARE

A studio that thinks in systems and ships in weeks.

We are a focused R&D team with a deep conviction: technology should feel calm, honest, and effortless. We design and build privacy-first software with a strong aesthetic point of view, blending procedural generation, immersive interaction design, and a relentless focus on user trust.

Every product that leaves our studio is engineered with the same care we would want for ourselves — minimal by default, secure by architecture, beautiful by intention. No dark patterns. No data harvesting. No compromises.

Apps published on the App Store

Six apps across six distinct problem domains — each built with a single guiding idea: the data you create belongs to you, and the tools you use should respect your time and your attention.

01

Local Workflow Automation

Chain native iOS and Android actions into powerful on-device scripts. No servers. No telemetry.

  • Visual flow editor
  • 40+ native triggers & actions
  • End-to-end encrypted sync
02

Collectibles Value Engine

Track, value and project the worth of your collectibles portfolio. Market data updated locally.

  • Multi-category support
  • Offline valuation models
  • CSV / JSON export
03

Posture Training Tracker

Sensor-driven, privacy-aware posture coaching. Your movement data never leaves the device.

  • On-device ML inference
  • Daily progress graphs
  • Apple Health & Health Connect
04

House Document Vault

Encrypted storage for deeds, contracts, warranties, insurance, and renovation records.

  • Biometric unlock
  • AES-256-GCM encryption
  • Reminders & expiry alerts

Inspiration Fragmenter

Capture fleeting ideas — voice, photo, sketch, link — and let the app tag, group and resurface them.

Period Budget Engine
